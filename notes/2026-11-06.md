This study aimed to investigate how periodontitis would alter bloodstream infections (BSIs) in patients undergoing allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (allo-HSCT). We hypothesized that periodontitis would alter the microbiological profile of BSIs, particularly the relative prevalence of viridans group streptococci (VGS), common oral bacteria frequently implicated in BSIs in allo-HSCT recipients, and other non-VGS organisms. We further investigated whether these microbiological changes were associated with clinical outcomes and alterations in VGS-specific humoral immune responses. A retrospective cohort (n = 489 patients, 2010-2020) and a prospective cohort (n = 149 patients, 2021-2022) were included for this observational study recruited from Princess Margaret Cancer Centre. Patients were categorized based on pretransplant history of periodontitis. BSIs occurring during the hospitalization period and mortality up to 1,000 days posttransplant were compared between patients with and without periodontitis.
