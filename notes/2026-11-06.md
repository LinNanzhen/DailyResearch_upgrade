OBJECTIVE: To investigate the effect of neoadjuvant chemotherapy (NACT) by evaluating its impact on surgical efficiency and postoperative morbidity in patients undergoing complex free-flap reconstruction for advanced oral cavity squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC). METHODS: A retrospective comparative study was conducted on 86 patients (NACT cohort: n = 20; Primary Surgery cohort: n = 66) undergoing microvascular free tissue transfer. Primary outcomes included surgical efficiency (ischemic time, total operative duration) and postoperative complications (major microvascular events vs. minor wound issues) that were evaluated. RESULTS: The NACT group presented with significantly more advanced disease (100% Stage IV vs. 39.4%; p < 0.001).
